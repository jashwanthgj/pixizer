How to use Pixizer's Advanced Resizer

Welcome to Pixizer's free online image resizer. Whether you need to resize by exact pixels, physical units (cm, mm, inches) for printing, or scale an image by percentage, this tool handles it all.

Understanding Resizing Modes

  • By Size (Pixels/CM/Inches): Best for specific requirements. Exam portals usually require pixels (px). If you are preparing a document for print, select Centimeters (cm) or Inches. Our tool automatically converts physical units to digital pixels using standard web 96 DPI.
  • As Percentage: The easiest way to maintain your photo's aspect ratio. Select "As Percentage" and type 50% to perfectly halve your image size without stretching or distorting it.
  • Smart Compression: Enter your target KB size. Our algorithm will compress the JPG or WebP image to fit under that limit.

100% Privacy Guaranteed

Unlike other tools that upload your personal photos to their servers, Pixizer works entirely inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas technology. Your files are never uploaded, ensuring total privacy and security.
