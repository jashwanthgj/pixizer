Welcome to Pixizer

Pixizer is a growing suite of digital tools designed to make your daily tasks easier. Whether you are a student preparing documents for government exam portals, a developer optimizing images for web performance, or just someone looking to save space on their phone, our platform is built for you.

Why Choose Pixizer?

  • Privacy First: Most online tools upload your sensitive photos to their cloud servers. Pixizer uses advanced HTML5 technology to process everything locally on your device. Your files are never uploaded.
  • No Watermarks: Completely free to use with no hidden fees, subscriptions, or annoying watermarks on your final images.
  • Lightning Fast: Because processing happens on your device's CPU rather than over a network connection, conversions and compressions happen instantly.
